About

The through-line in my work is measurement. Pipeline, dashboard, forecast, or retrieval system — the question is always the same. How do you know it is right?

That has meant three things. The platform: ETL/ELT in Azure Data Factory into Snowflake, dimensional models with a defined grain, schema contracts that reject bad records at load time. The reporting layer on top: Power BI semantic models, row-level security, one canonical definition per metric. And the models: time-series forecasting with rolling backtests at Accenture, and at Groflex AI a RAG pipeline scored against a labeled set rather than judged by feel.

I am finishing an MS in Information Systems at Northeastern, after nearly two years on the data side at Accenture and a co-op at Groflex AI.
