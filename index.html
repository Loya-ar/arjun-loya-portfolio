About

The through-line in my work is measurement. Pipeline, dashboard, forecast, or retrieval system — the question is always the same. How do you know it is right?

That has meant three things. The platform: Azure Data Factory pipelines into Snowflake, star-schema models, and SQL validation and reconciliation rules that stop bad data before it reaches a report. The reporting layer on top: Power BI with DAX and row-level security, and an 80+ KPI registry with one agreed definition per metric. And the AI layer: at Groflex AI, a RAG pipeline returning answers in 1–3 seconds with per-tenant isolation.

I am finishing an MS in Information Systems at Northeastern, after nearly two years on the data side at Accenture and a co-op at Groflex AI.

Chinook OLTP to Snowflake — Incremental ELT

Replicates the Chinook OLTP database into Snowflake with change data capture. Each row is hashed with SHA-256 to detect changes, and merge upserts apply them to SCD Type 1 dimensions. Extracts are staged as Parquet, then layered from staging to curated Snowflake tables, with secrets held in Azure Key Vault and validation counts checked at every layer.

Project Details

NYC Taxi Demand Forecasting — ARIMA in R

Forecasts hourly demand across 3.48M NYC yellow-taxi trips from January 2025 with ARIMA in R, tested with rolling 24-hour forecasts against simple baselines. ARIMA beat “same hour yesterday” by 15%, but a plain “same hour last week” forecast beat every model, because weekends differ sharply from weekdays and one month holds too few weeks to learn that. A day-by-hour heatmap maps demand, and a capped-supply simulation estimates overnight revenue at risk.
